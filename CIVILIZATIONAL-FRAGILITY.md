# Sociological Fragility

Civilizations behave like cognitive systems.

They hallucinate stability, suppress contradictions, and collapse when narrative complexity exceeds processing capacity.

---

## Collective Delusion

Societies maintain coherence through shared myths such as:

- guaranteed progress
- institutional competence
- economic permanence
- moral direction

These myths reduce existential panic.

---

## Social Entropy

Over time institutions accumulate instability:

- complexity rises
- trust erodes
- narratives fracture
- institutions become performative

Collapse occurs when the cost of coherence exceeds available social energy.

---

## Collective Resilience

Civilizations survive when they:

- adapt narratives
- acknowledge contradictions
- maintain flexible identity
- evolve faster than their illusions decay
