# Emotional Physics

Emotion is not abstract. It is the redistribution of internal energy within a biological system attempting to survive entropy.

---

## Core Principle

Emotions function as energy states regulating psychological stability.

Examples:

- Joy → temporary decrease in internal entropy
- Anxiety → predictive overload
- Grief → collapse of a shared regulatory system
- Love → synchronized entropy management

---

## The Economy of Hope

Hope functions as an energy loan taken against an imagined future.

When hope collapses, the psychological system experiences despair.

---

## Emotional Sovereignty

Humans cannot control which emotions arise, but they can influence how emotional energy is allocated.

Sovereignty emerges when individuals consciously regulate emotional thermodynamics rather than reacting automatically.
