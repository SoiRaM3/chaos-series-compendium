# The Stokknes Chaos Theory Compendium

A mythic-academic framework for understanding human collapse.

Author: M. J. Stokknes

---

## Overview

The Chaos Theory Compendium is the central philosophical framework underlying the Chaos Series.

It explores the structural forces shaping human existence through ten interacting domains of chaos spanning civilization, psychology, emotion, time, and reality.

The framework examines how individuals and societies attempt to maintain coherence within systems that naturally tend toward entropy and collapse.

---

## The Ten Domains of Chaos

The Compendium organizes human experience into ten structural domains:

### The World (Macroscopic Systems)

1. Civilization  
2. Entropy  
3. Faith  
4. Power  

### The Self (Microscopic Systems)

5. Identity  
6. Information  
7. Love  
8. Philosophy  

### The Meta Layer

9. Time  
10. Reality  

These domains describe the arenas where human meaning, identity, and stability are constructed and destabilized. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
