# Reality Maintenance

Reality is not perceived passively.

It is actively constructed and maintained through:

- perception
- emotional regulation
- narrative
- memory
- identity

Each subsystem consumes cognitive resources.

---

## Cognitive Budget

Human cognition operates under a limited energy budget.

When demands exceed capacity, individuals experience:

- confusion
- dissociation
- exhaustion
- reality drift

---

## Collapse Threshold

When the cost of maintaining a coherent world exceeds available energy, reality fragments and must be reconstructed.
