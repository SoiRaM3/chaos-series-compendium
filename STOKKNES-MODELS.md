# The Stokknes Models

The Chaos Framework introduces ten conceptual models describing how humans metabolize chaos.

Each model describes a structural mechanism shaping cognition, society, and meaning.

---

## Collapse–Rebuild Cycle (CRC)

Civilizations oscillate between periods of rigid structure and chaotic dissolution. Collapse is not failure but the reset mechanism for systems that accumulated more stability debt than they can service.

---

## Entropy Gradient Theory (EGT)

Entropy is the universal direction of disorder. Systems require continuous energy investment to maintain stability.

---

## Meaning Projection Mechanism (MPM)

Humans do not discover meaning — they project it onto otherwise indifferent phenomena.

---

## Power–Visibility Paradox (PVP)

Power strengthens as it becomes less visible. Participation replaces obedience.

---

## Identity Reinforcement Model (SIRM)

Identity is the accumulation of reinforced behaviors across developmental phases.

---

## Cognitive Overload Curve (COC)

Human cognitive capacity is finite. Once information exceeds processing capacity, individuals default to simplified heuristics.

---

## Attachment–Entropy Dynamic (AED)

Love stabilizes psychological systems by distributing emotional entropy across individuals.

---

## Meaning Construction Engine (MCE)

Meaning emerges through narrative compression and conceptual framing.

---

## Temporal Distortion Loop (TDL)

Human time perception varies with emotional state, memory, and anticipation.

---

## Reality Fracture Model (RFM)

When cognitive and emotional load exceed stability thresholds, reality coherence fractures and reconstructs.
