# The Ten Domains of Chaos

The Chaos Theory Compendium organizes human existence into ten interacting domains.

These domains represent the arenas in which human meaning, stability, identity, and perception are constructed and destabilized.

They are divided into three structural layers.

---

# I. The World (Macroscopic Chaos Systems)

These domains represent the external forces shaping individuals and civilizations.

## 1. Civilization
Civilization is the collective hallucination that order exists. Its purpose is not progress but the management of species-wide panic through systems of structure, ritual, and narrative.

## 2. Entropy
Entropy is the universal direction of decay. All systems require continuous energy to maintain stability.

## 3. Faith
Faith is belief in coherence. Humans rely on faith every time they assume tomorrow resembles today.

## 4. Power
Power is the architecture of permission — determining what individuals may do, believe, or become.

---

# II. The Self (Microscopic Chaos Systems)

These domains operate within the individual psyche.

## 5. Identity
Identity is not essence but reinforcement history — the accumulated pattern of rewarded behaviors.

## 6. Information
Information is both fuel and poison for consciousness. Modern collapse is driven by information overload exceeding cognitive bandwidth.

## 7. Love
Love is a thermodynamic process — two unstable systems regulating entropy together.

## 8. Philosophy
Philosophy is structured confusion — frameworks designed to contain chaos rather than eliminate it.

---

# III. The Meta Layer

These domains govern both the world and the self.

## 9. Time
Time is a psychological weather system shaped by emotion, memory, and anticipation.

## 10. Reality
Reality is a negotiated hallucination constructed through perception, cognition, and narrative.
